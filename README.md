## 🎮 About the Game

Inferno Protocol is a first-person co-op survival horror game developed and published by Tektonic Interactive. It entered Steam Early Access on August 28, 2026. Players explore an enormous, ever-changing labyrinth alone or with up to six players while scavenging, crafting, fighting creatures, and searching for an exit.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Inferno Protocol**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and survival assistance for Inferno Protocol, including protection from critical survival threats, improved resource management, faster crafting, and enhanced access to abilities. It is designed around the game's exploration, scavenging, crafting, combat, mutation, and maze-navigation systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the labyrinth with reduced survival pressure, experiment with crafting and mutations, practice combat, or enjoy a more relaxed solo or private co-op experience.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents normal damage from reducing the player's health during exploration and combat.
- **No Hunger and Thirst** — Stops hunger and thirst from increasing during a run.
- **Free Crafting** — Allows recipes to be crafted without consuming their required materials.
- **Instant Crafting** — Removes crafting time for faster preparation in the central settlement and other safe areas.
- **Mutation Protection** — Prevents unwanted mutation and deformation effects while retaining controlled access to beneficial abilities.

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents ammunition from being consumed when using firearms.
- **Unlimited Trap Supplies** — Provides an unlimited supply of deployable traps for defending corridors and ambushing creatures.
- **Unlimited Ability Charges** — Keeps syringe-granted abilities such as dash, rage, night vision, double jump, and extra health available for repeated use.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Infinite Resources** — Keeps scavenged crafting materials available for equipment, weapons, and defensive tools.

### 🌍 World

- **Safe Exploration** — Reduces the impact of environmental dangers and survival penalties while navigating the changing labyrinth.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer to reduce survival interruptions while moving through the circular labyrinth. Navigation remains based on memory, environmental landmarks, spray, chalk, notebooks, dropped objects, and found paper maps. Ability options can make movement tools such as dash and double jump available more consistently.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect scrap metal, wires, and harvested creature materials as usual. Infinite Resources preserves crafting supplies, while Free Crafting lets you produce weapons, gear, traps, and other equipment without consuming stored materials. Disable inventory-related options before trading if the game behaves unexpectedly.

### World & Environment

Inferno Protocol features a massive maze whose corridors and environments change as players progress. The central settlement provides farming, building, crafting, and trading opportunities, while smaller camps may offer interaction or commerce. Use Safe Exploration for extended scouting, but keep environmental markings and team communication central to navigation.

### Combat & Weapons

The game uses first-person firearms and improvised equipment against creatures that can react to sound and scent. Unlimited Ammunition supports extended firefights, while Unlimited Trap Supplies helps prepare defensive corridors. Ability and health options are best used for practice, difficult encounters, or private co-op sessions.
